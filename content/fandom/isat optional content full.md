hi! chances are you're a person who was told to go play In Stars and Time by someone you know, possibly by me (in which case hi heheh welcome to my webbed site). you got sent this page because they want you to get the most out of your first playthrough, so you don't have to replay unless you wanna be super completionist about it.

now i believe many story games are best played going in blind, and i still think that's true of ISAT, *but* there is a *lot* of optional content that's really important and easy to miss! i missed some of it on my first run and got partially spoiled for it, which wasn't great. but it's not something that's that easy to accidentally stumble upon. hence why i'm putting this together!

this page will contain a series of hints for things you should check out at certain points in the game. **In Stars and Time is split up into 6 Acts**, which will be the primary landmarks used throughout this guide.

**each time you start an Act, check its section on this page.** you can find out what Act you're on in the save menu, but each Act's section here will also tell you what to look out for when the next Act starts. Acts are pretty important to this game's mechanics, as they're the main factor in what things change when.

Act 1 doesn't have anything super important missable content in it, so we'll start off with Act 2.

> [!attention] ACT 2 LANDMARK
> Act 2 starts on Loop 1, after Siffrin's first death.

# ACT 2

here's your first to-do list! click/tap on these dropdowns to open them.

> [!done]- THINGS TO DO
> when you reach floor 3, there's a secret room with some cool stuff in it. some novels, a jackpot of tonics, and a note someone wrote to their mother. go look in there!

> [!hint]- HINT (DIRECTIONS)
> from the center of the third floor, go all the way to the right along the bottom. you'll find a room with a frozen girl and her dog. between the shelves and barrels on the right wall, there's some debris. there's a hole in the wall there. go through it and it will lead to a secret hallway. go through and you'll find a room. interact with everything in there.

> [!attention] ACT 3 LANDMARK
> Act 3 starts upon defeating the King.

# ACT 3

> [!done]- THINGS TO DO
> there are several things to do here, but none of them are *super* important. also, **all of these are available in Act 4 as well.**
> 1. Confession Event (awards Memory of Confession and the "Wait for it..." achievement)
> 	- get to the end and talk to everyone (Isabeau last) without completing all friendquests on four separate loops
> 2. Classroom Event (awards Memory of Learning and the "Teachable Moment" achievement)
> 	- on a full friendquest loop, interact with the blackboard in the classroom on floor 2

> [!attention] ACT 4 LANDMARK
> Act 4 starts after you've done all the friendquests at least once and have asked the King to stop fighting. you will get the "In this moment..." and "Nostalgie" achievements respectively.

# ACT 4

> [!done]- THINGS TO DO
> 
> 1. Bad Touch Event (awards Memory of Touch and the "Bad Touch" achievement)
> 	- talk to Isabeau in front of the Favor Tree enough to have asked him about the hand thing
> 	- finish the Confession Event
> 	- talk to Isabeau in front of the Favor Tree again. if you get the "(...)" and "(Keep waiting.)" options, choose "(...)". if you don't, try again next time.
> 2. Change God Event (awards Memory of Change God and the "but then who was phone???" achievement)
> 	- interact with 5 Change God statues in one loop.
> 	- after getting the KeyKnife, go back to the shrine room and interact with the Change God statue.

> [!attention] ACT 5 LANDMARK
> you'll know when Act 5 starts. trust me

> [!attention] ACT 5 LANDMARK
> Act 6 starts when Siffrin no longer has a hat.
