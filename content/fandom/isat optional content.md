hi! chances are you're a person who was told to go play In Stars and Time by someone you know, possibly by me (in which case hi heheh welcome to my webbed site). you got sent this page because they want you to get the most out of your first playthrough, so you don't have to replay unless you wanna be super completionist about it.

now i believe many story games are best played going in blind, and i still think that's true of ISAT, *but* there is a *lot* of optional content that's really important and easy to miss! i missed some of it on my first run and got partially spoiled for it, which wasn't great. but it's not something that's that easy to accidentally stumble upon. hence why i'm putting this together!

> [!info] note
> there are plenty of events and things that this guide skips! honestly, i'm mostly just trying to make sure people get the Act 6 thing, and that one Act 2 exclusive dialogue, because i think those are important and referenced enough that i'd like people to see them first-hand without getting them spoiled later.
> 
> that being said, i have a full optional content guide in the works that i can finish if requested!

this page will contain a series of hints for things you should check out at certain points in the game. **In Stars and Time is split up into 6 Acts**, which will be the primary landmarks used throughout this guide.

**each time you start an Act, check its section on this page.** you can find out what Act you're on in the save menu, but each Act's section here will also tell you what to look out for when the next Act starts. Acts are pretty important to this game's mechanics, as they're the main factor in what things change when.

Act 1 doesn't have anything super important missable content in it, so we'll start off with Act 2.

> [!attention] ACT 2 LANDMARK
> Act 2 starts on Loop 1, after Siffrin's first death.

# ACT 2

here's your first to-do list! click/tap on these dropdowns to open them.

> [!done]- THINGS TO DO
> when you reach floor 3, there's a secret room with some cool stuff in it. some novels, a jackpot of tonics, and a note someone wrote to their mother. go look in there!

> [!hint]- HINT (DIRECTIONS)
> from the center of the third floor, go all the way to the right along the bottom. you'll find a room with a frozen girl and her dog. between the shelves and barrels on the right wall, there's some debris. there's a hole in the wall there. go through it and it will lead to a secret hallway. go through and you'll find a room. interact with everything in there.

> [!attention] ACT 3 LANDMARK
> Act 3 starts upon defeating the King.

# ACT 3

> [!done]- THINGS TO DO
> 1. you should talk to Loop a lot! the "Let's talk about something." option specifically. it's fun! don't ignore them, they're lonely :< remember you can always use Tears to loop back!
> 2. have you ever checked out the boulangerie? they have some nice pastries there! i think one of their croissants is somewhere in the House as well, behind one of the openphrase doors. if you don't have all three openphrases, you should try exploring a bit to find them, and then find out what doors you can open with them! ...have you ever noticed how Siffrin seems to hate croissants? i wonder why that is...
> 3. there's someone who gives you a flower in Dormont if you talk to them... your first instinct is to give it to your allies, but is there anyone else you could give it to...?

> [!hint]- HINT (DIRECTIONS)
> 1. make sure to visit Loop a lot and talk to them, both throughout this Act and the next. they have some interesting dialogue! if you ever run out of conversation topics, go do some other things and come back later. they'll tell you when they're completely out of conversations. if you want to get all the conversations, make sure to loop back with Tears a bunch until Siffrin brings up how annoying they are. Tears aren't your only option, after all. you have an alternative on you already!
> 2. go visit the boulangerie in Dormont, and look for openphrases. one of them is Change, another is in the library in Dormont, and the last is on the third floor of the House behind one of the incorrect Tear-blocked paths. once you get that last one, there's an openphrase door on floor 2 you can open using it. there's a croissant in there. go take a look at it.
> 3. you can give the flower to Loop, the King, and the Head Housemaiden. Loop might take a few tries.

> [!attention] ACT 4 LANDMARK
> Act 4 starts after you've done all the friendquests at least once and have asked the King to stop fighting. you will get the "In this moment..." and "Nostalgie" achievements respectively.

# ACT 4

> [!done]- THINGS TO DO
> 1. remember that Silver Coin you have in your pocket? it's been with you this whole time, hasn't it? i wonder what would happen if you looked at it enough... i wonder if it interests Loop at all. maybe you could show it to them!
> 2. speaking of showing things to Loop, there's someone that gives you a free flower in Dormont that you could give to your family members... what if you gave it to Loop? they're important to you, right?
> 3. have Tears gotten annoying yet?

> [!hint]- HINT (DIRECTIONS)
> 1. interact with the Silver Coin in your pockets until you get a cutscene. once you do, go stand in front of the Favor Tree and interact with it there to show it to Loop.
> 2. try giving Loop the flower again.
> 3. if you've touched a Tear at least ten times, there's a chance of having a certain conversation with Loop. maybe try to get it if you haven't already. see Act 3's hint for this.

> [!attention] ACT 5 LANDMARK
> you'll know when Act 5 starts. trust me. it's the point of no return and even Siffrin knows that and will give you the option to stay in Act 4 a little longer.

# ACT 5

> [!done]- THINGS TO DO
> 1. i wonder how your profile looks. you can't tell what expression you're making. i wonder if others can tell.
> 2. if you plan on breaking free this time, you should gather all the souvenirs you can. be careful what you take a second look at.
> 3. remember all the things you did here? wasn't it nice? that closet, those plants, that diary. take a look at them. hold those memories tight while you still can.
> 4. one last bathroom monologue? pleaaaase?
> 5. no no no no no no it's fine
> 6. can't hurt them. attack the only person left.

> [!hint]- HINT (DIRECTIONS)
> 1. open your menu and check your profile. interact with all the NPCs possible in Dormont.
> 2. gather all the souvenirs you can. interacting with some of them will give you interesting dialogue, but interacting with others will destroy them. you'll get different dialogue interacting with them before and after the King.
> 	- **safe to interact with at all times:** Silver Coin, Bell Pendant, Broken Doll, Crumpled Poem, Shiny Piece of Glass (interact twice!), Lumpy Clay, Massive Chain, Mirror Picture (take a look at it!)
> 	- **interact with these after the King:** Bright Flower, Silver Coin, Bell Pendant, Broken Doll, Drawn Card, Lumpy Clay, Massive Chain, Shiny Piece of Glass
> 3. interact with everything you can remember there being dialogue with. maybe if you do so twice, the second one will be a bit different?
> 4. try to monologue in the bathroom until it happens.
> 5. when given the option, choose "(no no no no no no it's fine)". it's cute
> 6. in the battle after the King, take a look at your Craft skills. i'd recommend using that instead of your normal attack.

> [!attention] ACT 5 LANDMARK
> Act 6 starts when Siffrin no longer has a hat.

# ACT 6

> [!done]- THINGS TO DO
> 1. take a look through your pockets! you have a lot of stuff in there!
> 2. talk to everyone!!! your family members, and everyone in Dormont! not the Head Housemaiden yet. in fact, leave the House for now!
> 3. do you think Loop's still here? you'd... like to say thanks to them. **(make a save first and don't override it!!!)**
> 4. when you have them both, take a look at the coins in your pocket by the Favor Tree.
> 5. once you say goodbye, you can go talk to the Head Housemaiden. take a look at the coin again first!

> [!hint]- HINT (DIRECTIONS)
> 6. interact with every item in your pockets.
> 7. interact with your party, then leave the House and interact with everyone along the way. don't talk to the Head Housemaiden yet.
> 8. go to the Favor Tree to see if you can find Loop. make sure you make a save beforehand that you can go back to later, as there's two ways the following interaction can go.
> 9. at the Favor Tree, you'll pick up a coin. in your pockets, interact with yours and then the one you picked up.
> 10. once you tell your friends you said thanks to Loop, take a look at the coin in your pocket again, and then go back to the House to end the game by talking to the Head Housemaiden.
